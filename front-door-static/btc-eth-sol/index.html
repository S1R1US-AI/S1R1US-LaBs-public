Vitalik Buterin in his own words: the Ethereum roadmap

Vitalik Buterin co-founded Ethereum. Below are his own published words, quoted exactly and linked to his blog so you can read each one in full context. He has no connection to S1R1US.ai and does not endorse this page or our opinions. We quote him for commentary and satire. Read the roadmap lines, then the Bitcoin line, and decide for yourself which coin is trying to do one thing well.

So what's the result? Block production is centralized, block validation is trustless and highly decentralized, and censorship is still prevented.

Vitalik Buterin, “Endgame”, December 6, 2021

The profound benefit of the Ethereum rollup-centric roadmap is that it means that Ethereum is open to all of the futures, and does not have to commit to an opinion about which one will necessarily win.

Vitalik Buterin, “Endgame”, December 6, 2021

One of the inevitable consequences of this is that we are seeing a trend of layer 2 projects becoming more heterogeneous.

Vitalik Buterin, “Different types of layer 2s”, October 31, 2023

The term "web3" was originally coined by Ethereum cofounder Gavin Wood, and it refers to a different way of thinking about what Ethereum is: rather than seeing it, as I initially did, as "Bitcoin plus smart contracts", Gavin thought about it more broadly as one of a set of technologies that could together form the base layer of a more open internet stack.

Vitalik Buterin, “Make Ethereum Cypherpunk Again”, December 28, 2023

Bitcoin is trying to solve one problem, or at most two or three. Ethereum, on the other hand, has lots of sub-communities with lots of different focuses. There is no single dominant narrative.

Vitalik Buterin, “Make Ethereum Cypherpunk Again”, December 28, 2023

One can build a system that one calls a "layer 2", but which is actually a highly centralized system secured by a multisig, with no plans to ever switch to something more secure.

Vitalik Buterin, “Make Ethereum Cypherpunk Again”, December 28, 2023

This milestone represents a key transition in Ethereum's long-term roadmap: blobs are the moment where Ethereum scaling ceased to be a "zero-to-one" problem, and became a "one-to-N" problem.

Vitalik Buterin, “Ethereum has blobs. Where do we go from here?”, March 28, 2024

Fees are finally much lower; my statement from seven years ago that the internet of money should not cost more than five cents per transaction is finally coming true.

Vitalik Buterin, “Ethereum has blobs. Where do we go from here?”, March 28, 2024

Basically, Ethereum is no longer just a financial ecosystem.

Vitalik Buterin, “Ethereum has blobs. Where do we go from here?”, March 28, 2024

One important near-term debate in the Ethereum roadmap is the question of how much to increase the L1 gas limit.

Vitalik Buterin, “Reasons to have higher L1 gas limits even in an L2-heavy Ethereum”, February 14, 2025

Gas limits are easy to increase, but difficult to decrease - and even if you do decrease them later, the consequences to centralization may well be permanent.

Vitalik Buterin, “Reasons to have higher L1 gas limits even in an L2-heavy Ethereum”, February 14, 2025
