Why this inspired us

Freedom of conscience comes before every other freedom. People should be able to believe, doubt, pray or walk away without fear of the state or a mob. When reports show restrictions and hostility rising across regions and faiths, it tells us that the right to think for yourself is not safe by default. We study faith and philosophy as research and education, never to promote or attack any religion.

Opinion: Leaders who profit improperly

Why this inspired us

We are disappointed by politicians who profit improperly. Across parties and across decades, published reporting has raised questions about leaders and their families making money while in or near public office. Opinion: We require new ethical leadership to draft real legislative changes and stop the corruption. We can vote out politicians, and we will advocate for changes from all political parties.

We are non-political and not affiliated with any religious organizations or practices.

Opinion: Search gatekeepers = TYRANTS: search, AI answers and who gets found

When a few systems decide what billions of people find, whoever controls the gate controls the conversation. Courts and regulators in the U.S. and EU have ruled on search and ad-tech dominance, and research shows people click fewer links when an AI summary appears. Small creators and new ideas get buried.
